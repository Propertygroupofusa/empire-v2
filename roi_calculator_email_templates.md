## Best Practices

1. **Personalize the bracket sections** - Don't send generic templates
2. **Lead with the value** - "See your ROI in 60 seconds" is stronger than "check this out"
3. **Make it frictionless** - The calculator has no login, no forms, no sales pitch
4. **Follow up** - 3 emails over 7-10 days with variations (most responses come on follow-up 2-3)
5. **Track opens** - Use a link shortener with tracking if possible (Bitly, Rebrandly)
6. **A/B test subject lines** - The ROI angle vs pain-point angle (which converts better in your market?)

## How to Send at Scale

**Option 1: Manual + BCC (Fast)**
- Copy/paste template
- Personalize [BRACKETS]
- BCC to 5-10 prospects at a time
- Send over 2-3 days (avoids spam filters)

**Option 2: Email Automation (Scalable)**
- Use Lemlist, Outreach, Apollo, or HubSpot
- Upload CSV with prospect names/emails
- Set up templates with personalization
- Auto follow-up after 5 days
- Track opens/clicks

**Option 3: LinkedIn (Direct)**
- Copy shortened version of template
- Send as DM (higher response rate than cold email)
- Follow up with email if they respond
