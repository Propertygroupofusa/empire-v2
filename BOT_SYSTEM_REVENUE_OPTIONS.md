## 🎯 OPTION ANALYSIS (Ranked by Revenue Potential)

### **OPTION 1: Lead Research Service (HIGH POTENTIAL) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

**What it does:**
- Businesses request: "Find me 100 tech companies in Boston with 50-200 employees, get their decision-makers"
- Bot system claims the job
- Humans (or AI) does the research
- Compiles report with company name, website, LinkedIn profiles, emails
- Customer pays $500-2,000 per research batch
- Bot handles job queue and payments

**Revenue Model:**
- Price: $500-2,000 per research batch
- Your cost: $50-100 in human labor or API calls
- Margin: 80-95%
- Recurring: No (one-time per customer)

**Pros:**
- Real market demand (sales teams, recruiters need this constantly)
- Can use AI to augment human researchers
- Proven business model (companies like Hunter, RocketReach do this)

**Cons:**
- Requires human researchers (not hands-off)
- Lots of competition

### **OPTION 2: Transcription/Audio Processing (MEDIUM-HIGH) ⭐⭐⭐⭐**

**What it does:**
- Customers upload audio files → need transcription
- Bot queues the jobs
- Use OpenAI Whisper API or humans to transcribe
- Return transcript + formatted document

**Revenue Model:**
- Price: $0.10-0.50 per minute of audio
- 1 hour audio = $6-30
- Your cost: $0.006/min (Whisper API)
- Margin: 95%+

**Pros:**
- Completely scalable (AI does the work)
- Huge demand (podcasts, interviews, meetings, conferences)
- Can integrate with Zapier for automation

**Cons:**
- Competitive (Rev, Otter.ai, etc. dominate)
- Lower margins if customers expect human accuracy
