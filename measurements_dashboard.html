🔬 How to Read This Dashboard
P-Value < 0.05: Edge is statistically proven (not random luck). Only then scale capital.
Win Rate 55%+: Profitable strategy baseline. Combine with p-value for proof.
Profit Factor > 1.5: Gross profit is 1.5x gross loss. Healthy edge indicator.
Sharpe Ratio > 1.0: Return per unit of risk. Higher = better risk-adjusted returns.
Kelly Fraction: Optimal % of capital to risk per trade. Use Kelly/4 (quarter-kelly) for safety.
30+ Trades Needed: Statistical significance requires minimum 30 samples. Before that, edge is unproven.
