What this measures: for each coin the family tree bot is eligible to trade, this pulls real historical hourly candles from Coinbase and simulates buying/selling using the exact same rules the live bot uses (target %, -2% stop, breakeven ratchet, $3.75 peak-profit giveback, real fees) — then ranks coins by what that strategy would actually have returned. This does not change what the live bot buys; it's a read-only report to inform that decision.
