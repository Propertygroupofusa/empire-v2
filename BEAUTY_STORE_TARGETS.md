## HOW TO FIND EMAILS (If links don't work)

**Method 1: Website footer**
- Go to website
- Scroll to bottom
- Look for "Contact Us" or "Customer Service"
- Copy email address

**Method 2: Hunter.io (Free tier)**
- Go to hunter.io
- Enter domain (e.g., tower28beauty.com)
- Get all email addresses
- Use support@ or hello@ or contact@

**Method 3: Direct search**
```
Google: "[brand name] contact customer service email"
Google: "site:[domain.com] @[domain.com]"
```
