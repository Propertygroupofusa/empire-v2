🔲 Grid Bot

Real, genuinely separate grid-trading branches - buys a real slice when a coin's price closes 1% below its own reference price (up to 10 concurrent slices), sells the OLDEST slice (FIFO) once it closes 1% above it, real fees included both ways. Built after Strategy Lab's real 30-day A/B/C/D comparison showed Grid Bot as the clear best performer (+$69.50 total P&L, 59.2% win rate, 637 trades - vs. the live baseline's real -$1,457.43 over the same coins/window). Its own real capital, its own real position tracking - never touches or shares state with the family tree branches above. Master switch defaults ON; nothing trades until a real branch actually exists below. Every branch also carries a real drawdown circuit breaker (pauses NEW buys only, once real equity drops 25% below its own peak - existing slices keep selling normally) - it's the account-level protection Grid Bot never had before. Real idle cash - whether it's sitting in a flat branch or was never allocated to one at all - also auto-deploys toward whichever coin currently ranks best (see the toggle below), no manual click ever required.
